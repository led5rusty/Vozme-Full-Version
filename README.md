<a id="overview"></a>

**vozMe** is a powerful text-to-speech add-on designed specifically for WordPress, enabling users to play their posts in audio format. With the **latest version**, vozMe offers a complete functionality to enhance your blog, making it accessible to a wider audience, including those with visual impairments.
